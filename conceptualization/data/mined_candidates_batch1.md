## Horn, Bernd (2014) — "The Strategic Utility of Special Operations Forces", Canadian Military Journal 14(4):66-70. Canada. Both (military+academic).

- **[SOF, Canadian doctrine]** "Special Operation Forces are organizations containing specially selected personnel that are organized, equipped and trained to conduct high-risk, high value special operations to achieve military, political, economic or informational objectives by using special and unique operational methodologies in hostile, denied or politically sensitive areas to achieve desired tactical, operational and/or strategic effects in times of peace, conflict or war." (p.67) — quoted: CANSOFCOM, An Overview, DND 2008, p.7 — title: Special Operations Forces — basis: Organization*Operators*Role and Function
- **[SOF, WWII]** "special men, special training and special missions" (p.66) — reported general WWII definition — title: Special Operations Forces — basis: Operators*Organization*Role and Function

## Dobbie, Elliott V. K. (1944) — "The Word 'Commando'", American Speech 19(2):81-90. USA. Academic (philologist).

It corresponded most closely... to the English term militia. The commando was not the individual burgher, but the military unit of which he was a member" (p.81) — own — title: Other Units — basis: Organization*Operators
