About the Archive

This repository contains structured datasets supporting research on Israeli special operations forces, terrorism, and military operations from the British Mandate era (1920) through the present day. All data were originally curated in Airtable and exported for archival and replication. Files are UTF-8 encoded; some contain Hebrew text.

Cross-Dataset Linkages

Units ↔ Individuals: Join via unit-affiliation fields (unit name appears in both individuals_glossary and Units tables).

Units ↔ Operations: Operations databases list participating units by name.

NSSC ↔ Reprisals ↔ GTD: Linkable via date + location for triangulation and inter-coder reliability studies.
